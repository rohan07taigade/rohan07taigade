## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I am a third-year Computer Science engineering student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I don't just train models—I build complete, end-to-end systems around them. My focus lies in understanding the math behind the algorithms, writing efficient C++ for complex problem-solving, and deploying ML models seamlessly using modern full-stack tools. 

Whether it's exploring explainable AI or bridging the gap between a backend model and an interactive frontend, I am always looking for the next technical challenge to tackle.
